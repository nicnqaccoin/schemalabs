In this notice, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Schema Labs Ltd. The company is responsible for personal information handled through this website and its contact channels. See Company information for registration details.

Documents you enter

Schema and payload text is passed to a browser worker for validation or comparison. This release does not upload those documents, fetch remote references, or call an AI service.

Inputs are held in page memory and are not saved to cookies or browser storage. Reloading or closing the tab clears the workspace. Files you choose are read locally; a downloaded report is saved by your browser.

Website delivery

Cloudflare serves this website. Delivering site files requires ordinary network information, such as IP address, request time, requested URL and browser headers. Cloudflare may process service logs for delivery and abuse prevention under its own policies.

This release does not add advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Fonts and scripts are served from the site rather than requested from external font or script CDNs.

When you contact us

Email addressed to founder@schemalabs.co.uk is routed through Cloudflare Email Routing to a Gmail mailbox used for correspondence. Those services process the message. We use correspondence to respond to your request and improve the tools.

Avoid emailing confidential documents when an anonymized example is sufficient. You can request deletion of correspondence by contacting Schema Labs Ltd at the email address above.

Contact & changes

For privacy questions or correspondence requests, contact Schema Labs Ltd: founder@schemalabs.co.uk.

If the product adds accounts, persistence, analytics or AI processing, this notice will be updated before those features become part of the workflow.
